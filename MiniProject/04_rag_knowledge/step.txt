Implementation steps
1. Define a versioned chunk schema containing text, segment IDs, video ID, and time bounds.
2. Split captions on sensible boundaries while preserving links to original segments.
3. Validate non-empty text and configurable overlap limits.
4. Embed chunks and persist vectors with stable IDs and required metadata.
5. Mark a video ready only after indexing succeeds.
6. Implement scoped retrieval using video/channel/date filters and ownership when applicable.
7. Return ranked evidence with traceable chunk IDs and source URLs.
8. Implement full per-video chunk enumeration for complete summaries and comparisons.
9. Reindex changed transcripts and remove stale chunks on replacement or deletion.
10. Evaluate retrieval using questions with known supporting segments.

Acceptance criteria
Relevant evidence can be recovered with correct video IDs and timestamps. Filters exclude unrelated videos. Repeated indexing does not duplicate chunks, and embedding-model changes trigger an explicit index migration.
